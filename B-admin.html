Ordinance No. 2024-01: Noise Control

Enacted Jan 15, 2024 · Community Peace

Published▼
This ordinance regulates excessive, unnecessary, or unreasonably loud noise which disturbs the peace. Prohibited hours: 10:00 PM–6:00 AM weekdays, 11:00 PM–7:00 AM weekends.

Ordinance No. 2024-03: Waste Management

Enacted Mar 2, 2024 · Environmental

Published▼
Residents must segregate waste into biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and special categories. Garbage must be placed out only on designated collection days.

Ordinance No. 2025-04: Curfew for Minors

Enacted Apr 5, 2025 · Child Welfare

Published▼
Children below 15 must be home by 9:00 PM. Ages 15–17 must be accompanied after 10:00 PM.
