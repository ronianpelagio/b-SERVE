📜 Barangay Ordinances

Read and understand your community's rules and regulations

🔎

Ordinance No. 2024-01: Noise Control

Enacted Jan 15, 2024 — Community Peace

▼
This ordinance regulates excessive, unnecessary, or unreasonably loud noise. Prohibited hours are 10:00 PM–6:00 AM on weekdays and 11:00 PM–7:00 AM on weekends. Violators are subject to fines as prescribed by the barangay council.

Ordinance No. 2024-03: Waste Management

Enacted Mar 2, 2024 — Environmental

▼
Residents must segregate waste into biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and special categories. Garbage must be placed out only on designated collection days. Open burning is strictly prohibited. Littering in public areas is subject to fines.

Ordinance No. 2024-05: Anti-Loitering

Enacted May 10, 2024 — Peace & Order

▼
Minors under 18 are prohibited from loitering in public places without adult supervision between 10:00 PM and 5:00 AM. Barangay tanods may escort minors found in violation to the barangay hall for documentation and guardian notification.

Ordinance No. 2025-02: Animal Control

Enacted Feb 20, 2025 — Public Safety

▼
All pet owners must register animals with the barangay. Dogs must be kept on leash in public. Stray animals may be apprehended. Pet owners must ensure rabies vaccination for their animals.

Ordinance No. 2025-04: Curfew for Minors

Enacted Apr 5, 2025 — Child Welfare

▼
Children below 15 must be home by 9:00 PM. Ages 15–17 must be accompanied after 10:00 PM. Exceptions include school activities, medical emergencies, and employment. Negligent parents may be summoned by Lupong Tagapamayapa.
